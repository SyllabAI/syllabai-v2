- **FOUND AND FIXED: the echo-guard leak.** The battery exposed a real product defect: `applyingRemote` leaked FOREVER whenever a store's `apply` threw mid-span — the outer catch swallowed the throw, the inline add/delete pair never completed, and every later mirror for that key no-oped for the rest of the process (the single-process battery made it visible; the pre-existing store-law failures ride the same class).
