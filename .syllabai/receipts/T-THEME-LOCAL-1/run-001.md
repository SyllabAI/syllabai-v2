## The mirror law (why this is the exact reverse of the sweep's own migration)

The T-ACCOUNT-ONLY sweep adopted the device key once INTO the account (its
no-data-loss clause: "commits up only when the account has no theme of its
own") and retired the device key once the account copy was clean. This revert
mirrors both: the account doc adopts once INTO the device (only when the
device holds no record of its own) and the account copy retires once the
device record is clean. `packages/contracts` keeps `"theme"` in the
workspace-document vocabulary (FORBIDDEN to touch): existing rows must stay
readable for the one-time adoption, and the GET plane must never 400 on
legacy rows. Every account's row converges to the honest null after its
first post-revert login.

## Live behavior after this ships (the operator-visible contract)

- Picking a theme writes ONLY `localStorage["syllabai-theme"]` — no PUT to
  `/api/v1/me/documents/theme` on theme changes.
- Sign-out / account switch: the theme STAYS (the browser's, not the
  account's); two accounts on one browser now SHARE the browser's theme.
- A different browser/device starts at the system default until its own
  user picks a theme there.
- One-time per account: the first post-revert login on a browser without a
  theme record adopts the account's current theme (nobody's theme resets
  because of this revert), and that account's row is nulled server-side —
  after which the account plane is out of the theme's path entirely.
- The pre-paint bootstrap applies the stored theme before first paint —
  no light/dark flash on load (the flash the sweep accepted is gone).
