## Placement of record (why "above the navbar", not "inside the h-14 row")

- The buildathon allows "your website's navbar **or another prominent area
  near the top** of your app". The header row is packed: quick-nav joins at
  lg and the identity cluster rides ml-auto — a 94–135px wide badge has no
  reliable slack between lg and xl, and the 375px-fit law already governs
  the sm end. Embedding would force either an overflow risk or a
  multi-window visibility hack.
- The strip is an announcement-bar attached to the TOP of the navbar block:
  visible at EVERY width on EVERY route the header itself renders (landing
  `/`, `/demo`, dashboards, login, courses, reader, past papers…), directly
  adjacent to the navbar. Not sticky — the header keeps the sticky slot and
  the strip scrolls away with the page.
- The showcase landing (`/` and `/demo`, the judges' entry screen) carries
  the sticker a second time at hero size right under the intro paragraph.
- The paper-viewer route keeps its s146 header-stands-down contract: the
  strip lives under the same `!viewerRoute` guard, so the PDF stays the
  whole screen there.

  (opaque coverage 97.1%, center 255, corners 0). Verified visually on
  light and dark composites before staging.

  files use it). Alt text of record (agent experience):
