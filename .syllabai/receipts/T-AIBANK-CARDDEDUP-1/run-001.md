## THE LAW

A question SLOT (one paper + one question number) serves at most once per card:

- slot identity = `(paperId, external_ref)` — case/whitespace-insensitive, a slot is a number on a paper, not a spelling — else `(paperId, normalized stem, marks, command_word)` for the ref-less rows;
- first-wins in the SQL's own deterministic ORDER BY — the collapse never re-orders;
- cross-paper rows NEVER collapse — a regional variant or a second paper owning the same stem is a real bank row with its own disclosed paper ref;
- siblings stay untouched — the sibling line is the candidate DISCLOSURE surface; hiding a second `exam_papers` row would un-disclose;
- `total`, the limit cut and every marks sum read AFTER the dedup — a collapse frees a shown slot instead of eating one.
