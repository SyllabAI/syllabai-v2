   actions slot, on the shared PageHeader block.

   [paperId], revision-notes/[noteId], ugm/[ugmId] — the `sm:text-3xl` and
   `sm:text-[28px]` escalations dropped); guided-lessons/[noteId] keeps its
   icon row above the shared header; 6 learner/directory pages converted
