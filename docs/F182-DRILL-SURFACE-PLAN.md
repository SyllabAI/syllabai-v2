## 4. Policy decisions of the opening (each pinned by test)

- **P1 — draw unit = part.** The filed row says "5-part randomized draw"; the Kognity analog
  says "5-question" — the part is the honest unit (marks 1..5, MCQ or structured).
- **P2 — address = one spine spec-point code.** `4CH1-<code>`; the bank is the cross-product of
  both serving planes (official past-paper + topic/SME), disclosed per plane in the census.
- **P3 — fail-closed floor.** A drill is offered at an address iff its pool holds ≥ 5 anchored
  parts. Below the floor: no drill (never a padded or short draw). 157 addresses qualify today.
- **P4 — naive recency dedupe with a retakability override.** Recently-seen part ids are
  excluded while the fresh pool still covers a full draw; on thin fresh pools the retake draws
  from the full pool (`usedFallback: true` on the result). Strict exclusion would break the
  "retakable forever" law at exactly the addresses that need retakes most.
- **P5 — determinism.** `(bundle, address, recency, seed) → one draw`; mulberry32 seeded PRNG,
  Fisher–Yates over a part-id-sorted copy (ingestion order can never leak into a draw). A retake
  is a new seed — the fresh-draw guarantee.
- **P6 — zero stakes by construction.** The module *selects*; it writes nothing, emits no
  gradebook event, touches no clock. Attempt-evidence emission is the build PR's seam (F-055-
  shaped), not this opening's.
